Resume Optimization Tips
Better Summary
A professional summary should be 2-3 sentences highlighting your key qualifications, experience level, and career goals. Keep it concise and tailored.
Better Experience
Use action verbs and quantify achievements. Instead of "Responsible for..." try "Led team of 5 to increase sales by 25%."
Before
Responsible for managing a team and handling customer accounts.
After
Led a team of 8 account managers, driving 30% growth in customer retention and $2M in recurring revenue.
Better Skills
Group skills by category (Technical, Soft Skills, Tools) and include proficiency levels. Prioritize skills mentioned in target job descriptions.
Achievement Impact
Every bullet point should follow the formula: Action + Method + Result. Add numbers, percentages, and timeframes.
Weak
Improved website performance.
Strong
Optimized React components and implemented lazy loading, reducing page load time by 42% and improving Core Web Vitals scores.
